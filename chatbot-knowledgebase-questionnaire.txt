NAASIRTRAVEL CHATBOT KNOWLEDGE BASE 
==========================================================
Purpose: Gather everything needed to build a comprehensive, accurate knowledge base
for the Naasir AI travel assistant.


SECTION 1 — COMPANY & SERVICES
--------------------------------
1.  What is the full official name, registered address, and all contact channels
    (phone, email, WhatsApp, live chat hours)?

2.  What are your current business hours and timezone for customer support?

3.  Do you operate in multiple languages? If so, which ones should the chatbot support?

4.  What is the full list of tour/package types you currently offer
    (Hajj, Umrah, international tours, domestic, group, private, etc.)?

5.  Are there seasonal packages or limited-time offerings we should document?


SECTION 2 — PACKAGES & PRICING
--------------------------------
6.  What specific packages are currently available, and what does each include
    (flights, hotels, transport, meals, guides)?

7.  What are the price ranges or tiers for each package?
    (Even approximate ranges help the bot give useful answers)

8.  Are there group discounts, family rates, early-bird pricing, or loyalty rewards?

9.  What are your payment plan options (installment schedules, deposit amounts, deadlines)?

10. Which payment methods do you accept (credit card, Interac, bank transfer, cash, crypto)?

11. What is the full refund/cancellation policy per package type, with exact deadlines
    and percentages?

12. Are there any hidden fees or surcharges users commonly ask about
    (visa fees, baggage, airport transfers)?


SECTION 3 — BOOKING PROCESS
-----------------------------
13. Walk us through the complete booking process step-by-step from the user's perspective —
    what happens from first click to final confirmation?

14. What documents are required at each stage of booking (passport, vaccination proof, etc.)?

15. How far in advance must bookings be made for each package type?

16. Can users modify a booking after it's confirmed? What are the rules and fees?

17. How does group/family booking work — can one person book for multiple travellers?

18. What happens if a package sells out or a trip gets cancelled by NaasirTravel?


SECTION 4 — HAJJ & UMRAH SPECIFICS
-------------------------------------
19. What are the current Saudi Arabia visa requirements you handle for Umrah and Hajj
    pilgrims (specific documents, costs, processing times)?

20. What vaccinations are currently mandatory vs. recommended for Saudi Arabia travel?

21. Do you provide mahram documentation assistance for women travelling without a
    male guardian?

22. What are the exact ihram rules and dress code requirements you tell your clients?

23. Do you offer pre-departure orientation sessions or guides for first-time pilgrims?

24. Are there age restrictions for any Hajj or Umrah packages?

25. What is your stance on Hajj lottery/quota — do you handle government quota applications?


SECTION 5 — DESTINATIONS & TRAVEL INFO
-----------------------------------------
26. Which countries/cities do you currently offer tours to beyond Saudi Arabia?

27. For each destination, do you have standard advice on: visa requirements, best travel
    times, local customs, safety tips, currency, and language?

28. Do you partner with specific airlines, hotels, or ground transport providers?
    Can the chatbot name them?

29. Are there any destinations you specifically do NOT serve that users commonly ask about?

30. Do you provide travel insurance, or do you recommend specific providers?


SECTION 6 — CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS & PLATFORM
-------------------------------------------
31. What can users manage inside their account dashboard
    (bookings, dependants, documents, invoices)?

32. What is the process for resetting a password or recovering a locked account?

33. What are the rules around adding dependants — age limits, required documents,
    maximum per account?

34. Can users download invoices, booking confirmations, or visa letters from their account?

35. Is there a mobile app, or is the platform web-only?


SECTION 7 — POLICIES & LEGAL
-------------------------------
36. What is your full privacy policy in plain language — what data do you collect
    and how is it used?

37. Are there any terms of service points users frequently misunderstand or ask about?

38. What is your complaint or dispute resolution process?

39. Are you registered with any travel industry bodies (TICO, IATA, ATOL, etc.)
    that give users confidence?

40. Do you have any age restrictions (minimum age to book independently, senior travel rules)?


SECTION 8 — COMMON CUSTOMER QUESTIONS (MOST IMPORTANT)
---------------------------------------------------------
41. What are the TOP 10 QUESTIONS your customer support team gets asked most frequently?

42. What are the TOP 5 COMPLAINTS OR MISUNDERSTANDINGS customers have that the chatbot
    could prevent?

43. Are there any questions you want the chatbot to ALWAYS REDIRECT TO A HUMAN AGENT
    rather than answer itself?

44. Are there sensitive topics (pricing disputes, complaints, medical emergencies) where
    you want the bot to immediately hand off to staff?

45. What should the chatbot say when it genuinely doesn't know the answer — is there a
    specific contact name, email, or phone number to direct users to?


SECTION 9 — CONTENT & DOCUMENTS YOU ALREADY HAVE
---------------------------------------------------
46. Do you have an existing FAQ document, employee training manual, or help centre
    we can use as source material?

47. Are there any brochures, itineraries, or package detail PDFs we can feed into
    the knowledge base?

48. Do you have blog posts, destination guides, or articles on your website we can index?

49. Are there any WhatsApp or email templates your support team uses that show the
    "right" answers to common questions?

50. Who on your team will be responsible for reviewing and updating the knowledge base
    going forward?


SECTION 10 — FUTURE & GROWTH
------------------------------
51. Are you planning to add new destinations or package types in the next 6–12 months?

52. Do you want the chatbot to eventually be able to check real-time booking status
    from your database?

53. Should the chatbot eventually handle booking initiation (collect user details and
    start a booking), or stay advisory-only?

54. Do you want analytics on what questions users are asking most — to identify
    knowledge gaps?

55. What languages do most of your customers speak (English, Arabic, French, Urdu, etc.)?


==========================================================
NOTE: The most critical sections are 2, 3, 4, and 8 — pricing/policies, booking
process, Hajj/Umrah specifics, and the real FAQ from your support team. Those four
alone will cover approximately 80% of what users ask the chatbot.
==========================================================
